# Mars analog "families" already well used (so "new" must differ)

Written alongside `known_mars_analogs_additions.csv`. Each family = Earth environment type that is already a standard Mars analog, with the canonical sites that anchor it. A candidate that merely sits in one of these environments (hyperarid Atacama core, Dry Valleys, Svalbard, Rio Tinto, Utah desert, etc.) is NOT new; it must differ in geology/climate/region from the canonical sites listed here.

- **Hyperarid cold/hot desert soils (Atacama-type):** Yungay and the Atacama core, Salar de Atacama, Maricunga, Pajonales, Laguna Lejia, Llullaillaco, Puna/Altiplano salars. Used for UV, oxidants, low water activity, perchlorate and life-detection tests (Chilean salars as evaporite analog: Flahaut et al. 2017, Icarus 282). The whole Chilean/Argentine Altiplano-Puna salar belt is saturated with prior work.
- **Polar/cold-desert ice and permafrost:** McMurdo Dry Valleys (Wright, Taylor, Beacon, University Valley, Lake Vanda/Bonney/Hoare, Don Juan, Blood Falls), Devon Island (Haughton/FMARS), Axel Heiberg (Gypsum Hill, Lost Hammer), Svalbard (AMASE, Bockfjorden), Lena Delta, Ladakh/Tso Kar/Hanle. Standard for ground ice, brines, periglacial landforms and cold-water mineralogy.
- **Acidic sulfate/iron-oxide systems (Meridiani-type):** Rio Tinto (MARTE, Pena de Hierro), Western Australian acid saline lakes (Lake Whurr/Orr/Brown/Tyrrell, Ruecker 2016), Dallol/Danakil, Wai-O-Tapu, Kawah Ijen-type acid lakes, Iceland Kverkfjoll/Askja sulfate hydrothermal systems. Hematite concretions near Hanksville/Moab (Utah) are the other Meridiani "blueberry" analog.
- **Evaporite playas, salt flats and gypsum dunes:** Salar de Uyuni, Bonneville, Dalangtan (Qaidam), Chott el Djerid, Lake Eyre, Lake Tyrrell, Dead Sea, White Sands/Estancia/Guadalupe gypsum, Cuatro Cienegas, Death Valley and Mojave dry lakes (desiccation polygons, El-Maarry 2015), Western Sahara sebkhas. Lacustrine/clay-sulfate stratigraphy analogs for Gale/Jezero are drawn almost entirely from this group.
- **Basaltic volcanic terrain, lava tubes and glaciovolcanism:** Hawaii (Mauna Kea PISCES, Kilauea/Mauna Ulu BASALT, HI-SEAS), Craters of the Moon, Lanzarote/Timanfaya, Teide, Iceland (Askja, Holuhraun, Lambahraun), Mt Etna, Tolbachik, Erebus, Saudi harrats and El Malpais lava fields. Used for rover/EVA operations, lava-tube cave habitats and weathering of Mars-like basalt.
- **Aeolian landforms (dunes, yardangs, TARs):** Namib sand sea, Dumont/Kelso/Algodones/Great Sand Dunes/White Sands, Qaidam and Lop Nur yardangs, Dasht-e Lut kaluts and ripples, Taklimakan, Great Sand Sea/Gilf Kebir/White Desert, Dyngjusandur sand sheet, Rub' al Khali.
- **Impact craters and impact-hydrothermal sites:** Haughton, Lonar (basaltic target), Meteor Crater, Sudbury, Gosses Bluff, Ries-type structures, Pingualuit, Wabar. Used for crater hydrothermal and shock-metamorphism analogs.
- **Operational habitats and rover test sites:** MDRS (Hanksville), FMARS, HI-SEAS, SAM/Biosphere 2, Mars-500/SIRIUS, LunAres, Lenghu and Jinchang Mars camps, Mars Science City (Dubai), Wadi Rum (PETRA), Ramon crater (AMADEE-20), Morocco/Erfoud (MARS2013, ESA rover trials), Oman Dhofar/Marmul (AMADEE-18), Kaunertal glacier (AMADEE-15), Armenia Ararat (AMADEE-24), JPL Mars Yard, Mars-Oz Arkaroola (proposed).
- **Subsurface/chemosynthetic and cave analogs:** Lechuguilla, Movile, Villa Luz, Boulby and Mponeng mines, lava tubes. Standard for subsurface life and sealed-habitat studies.

## What a genuinely "new" site must do
- Differ from the canonical sites above by **region and geology** (e.g. an unstudied sulfate-bearing playa or clay-rich lacustrine basin in an under-surveyed desert), not just by lat/lon.
- Prefer environments with **no existing Mars-analog literature** (Central Asian deserts, Karakum/Kyzylkum/Ustyurt, Jordan/Saudi playas, Libya/Algeria sebkhas, Tibet/Hoh Xil interior basins, Patagonian plateau lakes).
- Remember the catalog is a **blocklist**: low-confidence rows in `known_mars_analogs_additions.csv` are included on purpose so that a candidate near them is flagged for manual review rather than being claimed as new.
